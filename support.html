KADENCE Support

KADENCE is a taekwondo timer and training journal with spoken combination callouts.

No sound?

  • Check that the side ringer switch shows no orange, and turn the media volume up.
  • In Settings, make sure "Play in silent mode" is on, then use the play button next to the bell and "Test voice".
  • Fully close and reopen the app.

My workouts are missing

Workouts only count when you choose "Log it" at the end of a session. Your data lives on your phone, so deleting the app also deletes it.
