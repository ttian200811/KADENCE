KADENCE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

KADENCE is a taekwondo training timer. It is built to work entirely on your own device, and it does not collect your personal information or send your workout data anywhere.

What stays on your device

Everything you create in the app is stored only on your phone: your name, profile picture and home background photo, workout log and notes, statistics, achievements, saved combinations, custom kicks and settings. We do not have access to any of it, and it is not uploaded anywhere.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • No third-party services that receive your workout data. The only exception is the purchase check described below.
  • We do not sell or rent information.

Purchases

Some features are part of KADENCE Pro, which is bought through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and we never see your payment details. To confirm what you have bought and to restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. It receives an anonymous random identifier and your purchase status from Apple. It does not receive your name, email, workouts, photos or any other content from the app.

Photos

If you choose a profile picture or background photo, the app reads only the picture you pick and saves a resized copy on your device. The photo is never uploaded.

Sharing from the app

The "Share this workout" feature creates an image on your device. It only leaves your phone if you choose to paste or save it somewhere, such as Instagram, and that is covered by that app's own policy.

Voice callouts

Spoken callouts use the text-to-speech voices built into your phone. No audio is recorded and the microphone is not used.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. We hold no copy.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
