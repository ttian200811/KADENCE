How callouts work

Callout mode reads kick combinations aloud. The number you set is the total kick count: Cut-flip, Cut-hook, Cut-axe, Body-head and Double round each count as 2, Triple round counts as 3.

Focus areas

  • Front Leg: lead-leg setups that flow into power kicks.
  • Back Leg: rear-leg and spinning power kicks.
  • Counter: shift back and counter kicks.
  • Clinch: close range. Push off, then kick out of the break.
  • Overall: random, but only valid kick-to-kick transitions.

Legal targets (WT)

  • Kicks go to the trunk protector or the head only. No kicks below the waist.
  • Punches go to the trunk protector only. No punches to the face.
  • No grabbing or holding the opponent.

Olympic sparring scoring

  • Punch to the body: 1 point
  • Kick to the body: 2 points
  • Turning (spinning) kick to the body: 4 points
  • Kick to the head: 3 points
  • Turning (spinning) kick to the head: 6 points

Gam-jeom (penalties)

Each gam-jeom gives the opponent 1 point: crossing the boundary line, falling down, avoiding or delaying the match, grabbing or pushing, lifting the leg without attacking, attacking below the waist, and attacking after the break. In the final 10 seconds some of these, such as falling to avoid the fight, give the opponent 2 points. Source: superiortkd.co.nz, so check your own federation for the latest rules.
